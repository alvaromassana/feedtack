# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small tool maintained by one person in the gaps between client work.
Assume good faith, keep it about the code, and don't make anyone regret opening the issue.

## What's expected

- Talk about the change, not the person who proposed it.
- Take "no" without a fight. Most rejections here are about keeping the project small, not
  about the quality of your idea.
- Remember that most people writing here are not doing it in their first language, this
  project included.

## What isn't

Harassment, personal attacks, insults, trolling, publishing anyone's private information,
sexual attention or advances, and any behaviour that would be out of place in a working
relationship. Same standard in issues, pull requests, commit messages and private email.

## Scope

Anywhere the project is represented: this repository, its issues and pull requests, and
communication about it.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs it to **alvaro.massana@websalia.com**. Reports stay private.

The maintainer will look at it and decide, in this order: a private word, an edited or
deleted comment, a temporary block, a permanent one. Decisions get explained to the person
reporting. There is no committee and no appeals process; if you think a decision was wrong,
say so by email.
